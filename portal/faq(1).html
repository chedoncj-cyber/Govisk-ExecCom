Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Govisk ExecCom Intelligence platform.

What is Govisk ExecCom Intelligence?

Govisk ExecCom Intelligence is an AI-driven geo-financial risk and regulatory intelligence platform designed for executive leadership teams at global institutions. The platform synthesises macroeconomic trends, regulatory developments, geopolitical risk, and governance intelligence into structured, board-ready reports.

It operates through the WAT Framework — Workflows, Agents, Tools — which separates regulatory knowledge from AI reasoning and deterministic execution. This architecture enables high-accuracy, citation-backed intelligence across complex multi-jurisdictional topics.

Who is Govisk ExecCom Intelligence designed for?

The platform serves senior executives, compliance officers, risk managers, board members, and in-house legal teams at institutions operating in complex regulatory or geopolitical environments. Our primary client sectors include:

  • Financial services — banks, custodians, asset managers, broker-dealers, investment firms
  • Energy and resources — oil and gas, renewables, mining, commodities trading
  • Technology — enterprise software, cloud infrastructure, semiconductor, telecoms
  • Logistics and construction — shipping, supply chain, infrastructure development

Govisk ExecCom serves over 250 global institutions across six regions: UK, North America, Latin America, Asia-Pacific, Africa, and Europe.

Is Govisk ExecCom Intelligence FCA regulated? Are the reports financial advice?

No. Govisk ExecCom Intelligence Ltd is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). The intelligence reports, briefings, and analyses produced by the platform are for informational purposes only. They do not constitute regulated financial advice, investment recommendations, legal advice, or any other form of professional advisory service under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (FSMA).

Users should seek independent professional advice appropriate to their specific circumstances before making decisions based on intelligence outputs. For full details, see our Terms & Conditions.

How often are intelligence reports updated?

Intelligence reports are generated on demand — each report is freshly synthesised at the time of your request. Nine AI connectors simultaneously scan live regulatory publications, enforcement databases, and geo-financial data sources in real time before each report is produced.

Scheduled intelligence feeds (such as weekly regulatory digests and live regulatory tickers) are updated continuously. Bespoke report types — such as CASS Health Checks, Cyber Threat Assessments, and Country Risk Briefings — reflect the most current regulatory and risk environment at the time of generation.

How do I request a demo?

Visit our Request a Demo page and complete the short form. You will be asked for your name, job title, company, and a brief description of your intelligence needs. A member of our team will contact you within one business day to arrange a 30-minute live walkthrough of the platform.

Alternatively, contact us directly at hello@govisk.com.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect personal and organisational data. These include:

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS) across all platform endpoints
  • Role-based access controls limiting data access to authorised personnel
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments
  • Minimal data collection — we only hold what is necessary to deliver the service

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and process data in accordance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

What regulatory frameworks and regions does the platform cover?

The platform covers 13 intelligence report types across six global regions. Key regulatory frameworks include:

  • UK: FCA CASS 6 & 7, SM&CR, PS21/3 (Operational Resilience), CBEST, OFSI sanctions
  • Europe: ESMA MiFID II, DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act), MAR (Market Abuse Regulation), SFDR
  • Asia-Pacific: MAS Singapore guidelines, APRA operational resilience, JFSA
  • North America: SEC Regulation Best Interest, CFTC, FINRA, OCC
  • Africa: SARB, NSE, JSE
  • Latin America: B3 (Brazil), CMF (Chile), Banxico

Report types include CASS Health Checks, Cyber Threat Assessments, FCA Incident Response, Country Risk, Executive Protection (SM&CR), Due Diligence, and more.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time by emailing billing@govisk.com with your subscription reference and name. New subscribers may cancel within 14 days for a full refund under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

After the 14-day period, monthly subscriptions cancel at the end of the current billing cycle. Annual subscriptions are subject to our pro-rata refund policy for unused complete months. For full details, see our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
