Case Studies

From regulatory exposure to board-ready response

🇬🇧 UK · CASS 7 · Mid-Tier Custodian
Client Money Segregation Audit — £4.2B Custody Book
A Tier 2 FCA-regulated custodian received a Section 166 skilled person review notice citing concerns about client money reconciliation controls across three pooled accounts. The compliance team needed a structured gap analysis before the skilled person's initial meeting.
Outcome
CASS Health Check and Client Money Segregation Audit reports generated within 4 hours. Reports mapped 11 reconciliation deficiencies to specific CASS 7.13-7.16 rules, provided a prioritised remediation timeline, and gave the SMF16 holder a structured board narrative. Section 166 scope was narrowed following the submission.
🌎 North America · AML · Global Bank
FinCEN Examination Preparation — Cross-Border AML Programme
A global bank's US subsidiary received a 90-day FinCEN examination notice covering its AML transaction monitoring programme for correspondent banking relationships. The compliance officer needed a rapid assessment of current controls against BSA/AML expectations.
Outcome
Threat Intelligence (Banking) and Due Diligence reports scoped to North America identified 8 control gaps in the current SAR filing programme and correspondent bank risk tiering. The GRC Intelligence Report provided a single consolidated view for the CMO's examination presentation. All findings were remediated within the 90-day window.
🇪🇺 Europe · DORA · Asset Manager
DORA ICT Resilience Gap Analysis — Pan-European AM
A pan-European asset manager with AUM of €18B needed to demonstrate DORA compliance to BaFin and AMF by the January 2025 application date. Their existing BCM framework had not been mapped to DORA's five pillars: ICT risk management, incident reporting, testing, third-party risk, and information sharing.
Outcome
Cyber Threat Assessment and FCA Incident Response reports scoped to Europe generated a DORA pillar-by-pillar gap register. The Executive Protection (SM&CR) report adapted to identify MiCAR counterparty obligations. Reports were submitted to both regulators as part of the DORA compliance attestation — no further queries raised.
🌍 Africa · FATF · Regional Bank
FATF Mutual Evaluation Preparation — West African Bank
A Tier 1 West African bank faced FATF's 4th-round mutual evaluation of their jurisdiction's AML/CFT regime. The group compliance function needed to assess the bank's individual programme against FATF Recommendation 26 (bank supervision) and demonstrate technical compliance.
Outcome
Country Risk and Threat Intelligence reports scoped to Africa provided a jurisdiction-level FATF compliance scorecard. The Due Diligence report assessed 40+ correspondent relationships against GAFILAT and ESAAMLG expectations. The bank's FATF submission was rated technically compliant in 36 of 40 recommendations — the strongest result in the evaluation cohort.
🇬🇧 UK · SM&CR · Investment Firm
SMF Accountability Mapping — FCA Authorisation Application
A new market entrant applying for FCA authorisation as an investment firm needed a complete SM&CR governance framework — SMF role definitions, conduct rule training records, and a Statements of Responsibility for the six proposed Senior Managers.
Outcome
Executive Protection (SM&CR) and GRC Intelligence reports produced a full governance pack including role maps, conduct rule certificates, and a management responsibilities map. The FCA authorisation was granted within 8 months — 3 months ahead of the anticipated timeline. No requests for further information were received on the SM&CR documentation.
🌏 Asia-Pacific · MAS · Fund Administrator
MAS Technology Risk Guidelines — Singapore Fund Admin
A Singapore-licensed fund administrator with operations in Hong Kong and Tokyo needed to evidence compliance with MAS Technology Risk Management Guidelines (TRM 2021) ahead of a scheduled MAS inspection. Existing documentation covered ISO 27001 but had not been mapped to MAS TRM's specific requirements.
Outcome
Cyber Threat Assessment and Physical Security Audit reports scoped to Asia-Pacific produced a TRM-specific gap register with 24 control observations mapped to MAS TRM sections 1-10. The CASS Health Check adapted to Singapore Securities and Futures Act custody rules identified three additional custody control improvements. MAS inspection completed with no adverse findings.
