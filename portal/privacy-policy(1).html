Privacy Policy

Govisk ExecCom Intelligence  ·  Data Controller: govisk.com  ·  Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Who We Are

Govisk ExecCom Intelligence ("Govisk", "we", "us", "our") is the data controller for personal data collected through this website and the intelligence portal at govisk.com. We are a geo-financial risk and regulatory intelligence firm headquartered in London, United Kingdom.

Contact us regarding data privacy at: privacy@govisk.com

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact and account data: Name, email address, company/organisation name, and job title — provided when you request a demo, submit an enquiry, or create an account.
  • Usage data: Page views, session duration, browser type, and referring URLs, collected via analytics tools to improve the platform.
  • Technical data: IP address and request metadata, retained in server logs for security and operational purposes.

We do not collect any special category data (sensitive personal data as defined under UK GDPR Article 9). Our reports contain publicly available regulatory information only — no personal financial data relating to our users.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to demo requests and enquiries (lawful basis: legitimate interests / performance of a contract).
  • To deliver the intelligence platform and reports you have subscribed to (lawful basis: performance of a contract).
  • To improve platform functionality and user experience through anonymised analytics (lawful basis: legitimate interests).
  • To comply with applicable law and regulatory obligations (lawful basis: legal obligation).

4. Third Parties

We may share data with the following categories of third parties:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com): Serves web fonts. When fonts are loaded, your IP address may be transmitted to Google's servers. We load fonts via a Content Security Policy that restricts unauthorised connections.
  • Analytics providers: Anonymised usage statistics may be processed by our analytics tools. No personally identifiable data is shared for advertising purposes.
  • AI providers: When generating intelligence reports, queries may be processed by AI providers (Perplexity, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, Gemini, Cohere and others). No personal data about subscribers is included in these queries.
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers: Server and application hosting providers based in the UK/EEA or subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

  • Form submissions (demo requests, contact forms): retained for 2 years.
  • Server and access logs: retained for 90 days.
  • Account data: retained for the duration of your subscription and 12 months thereafter, unless you request erasure.

6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your data where there is no compelling reason for its continued processing.
  • Right to restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@govisk.com. We will respond within one calendar month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

7. Cookies

We use essential session cookies required for platform functionality. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when changes were last made. Continued use of the platform following an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
