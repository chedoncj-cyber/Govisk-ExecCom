Refund Policy

Govisk ExecCom Intelligence  ·  Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Subscriptions

Monthly and annual subscriptions may be cancelled at any time via your account dashboard. Cancellation stops future billing; access continues until the end of the current billing period.

2. Refund Window

In accordance with the Consumer Rights Act 2015, we offer a 14-day refund window from the date of purchase for subscription products. To request a refund within this window, contact billing@govisk.com with your order reference. Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method.

After 14 days, subscriptions are non-refundable but can be cancelled to prevent future charges.

3. Enterprise Contracts

Enterprise and institutional contracts are governed by the individual Service Level Agreement (SLA) negotiated between the parties. Refund and cancellation terms in those agreements take precedence over this policy.

4. One-Off Reports

One-off intelligence reports purchased outside of a subscription are non-refundable once the report has been delivered. If a report is not delivered due to a service error on our part, a full refund will be issued automatically.

5. Contact

For billing and refund queries: billing@govisk.com or visit the Contact Us page.
