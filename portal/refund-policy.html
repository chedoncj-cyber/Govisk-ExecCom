Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026  ·  Effective: 5 November 2026

Quick summary: New subscribers may cancel within 14 days for a full refund under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. After 14 days, pro-rata refunds apply for unused full months. All cancellation requests should be sent to billing@govisk.com.

1. Scope

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to all subscriptions to the Govisk ExecCom Intelligence platform purchased directly from Govisk ExecCom Intelligence Ltd. It is intended to be fair, transparent, and compliant with applicable consumer protection legislation in the United Kingdom, including the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.

2. 14-day cooling-off period

If you are a consumer (an individual acting for purposes wholly or mainly outside your trade, business, craft, or profession), you have the right to cancel your subscription within 14 calendar days of the date your subscription commences, without giving any reason.

To exercise this right, you must inform us of your decision to cancel before the 14-day period expires. You can do this by:

  • Emailing billing@govisk.com with your name, subscription reference, and a clear statement that you wish to cancel
  • Using our contact form at /contact

If you cancel within the 14-day period, we will refund all payments received from you, using the same payment method used for the original transaction, within 14 calendar days of receiving your cancellation request.

Note: If you requested that the service begin immediately during the 14-day period (e.g. by accessing the platform), we may deduct a pro-rata amount proportional to the service already provided before you exercised your cancellation right, in accordance with Regulation 36 of the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.

3. Cancellation after 14 days — corporate subscriptions

For annual or multi-month subscription agreements (typically held by corporate clients), cancellation after the 14-day cooling-off period is governed by the terms of the individual subscription agreement. Where no specific terms are agreed:

  • You may cancel at any time by providing 30 days' written notice to billing@govisk.com
  • Upon cancellation, we will issue a pro-rata refund for any complete calendar months remaining in the paid subscription period that have not yet commenced
  • Partial months already in progress at the time of cancellation are not refundable
  • No refund is issued for any months already delivered and accessed

4. Monthly rolling subscriptions

For monthly rolling subscriptions, you may cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. No refund is issued for the current month's subscription fee already paid. Access continues until the end of the paid billing period.

5. Non-refundable circumstances

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Requests made outside the applicable cancellation window where no pro-rata entitlement applies
  • Subscriptions terminated due to a breach of our Terms and Conditions
  • One-time bespoke report commissions where work has already commenced
  • Requests made on the basis of dissatisfaction with intelligence content that falls within our normal scope and quality standards

6. Service failures and remedies

If the Service has been unavailable for a continuous period exceeding 48 hours due to a failure on our part (excluding scheduled maintenance or force majeure events), we will credit your account with a proportional extension of your subscription period. Where a remedy in kind is not possible, a pro-rata refund for the affected period may be issued at our discretion.

7. Disputes

If you believe you are entitled to a refund and your request has been declined, you may escalate the matter by contacting us in writing at billing@govisk.com. We will acknowledge your dispute within 5 business days and aim to resolve it within 20 business days.

If your dispute remains unresolved, you may refer it to an Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) scheme or pursue it through the courts of England and Wales.

8. Contact

For all refund and cancellation requests, contact our billing team at billing@govisk.com. Please include your subscription reference number, name, and the reason for your request to expedite processing.
