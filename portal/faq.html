Govisk ExecCom Intelligence  ·  FCA · CASS · SM&CR · Cyber
Govisk ExecCom Intelligence is an AI-driven regulatory intelligence platform built for UK and global financial services professionals. It delivers FCA/CASS compliance reports, GRC dashboards, threat intelligence briefings, and real-time regulatory monitoring across six global regions. Reports are generated using our proprietary WAT framework (Workflows, Agents, Tools), which combines regulatory knowledge with live AI scanning.
Reports are generated through our WAT Framework: regulatory Workflows define the structure and compliance requirements; AI Agents (powered by Perplexity, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, Gemini, Cohere and others) scan live regulatory publications and enforcement data; and deterministic Tools format the output into structured, citeable intelligence documents. Each report includes RAG (Red/Amber/Green) risk ratings and FCA Handbook references.
Govisk ExecCom Intelligence is an intelligence and information service, not a regulated financial services firm. Our reports provide information and analysis aligned with the FCA Handbook, but they do not constitute regulated advice. Firms should consult their qualified compliance officers or FCA-authorised advisors when making compliance decisions. See our Terms & Conditions for the full disclaimer.
The live intelligence ticker and regulatory feeds are updated continuously. Report generation uses AI agents that scan live FCA publications, PRA releases, enforcement notices, and international regulatory sources at the time of generation. The platform references the FCA Handbook as of October 2026 for CASS, SM&CR, and PS21/3 requirements. Users should always verify against the current FCA Handbook for the most up-to-date rules.
Our primary coverage includes: CASS 6 (custody rules), CASS 7 (client money segregation), CASS 7A (client money distribution), CASS 10 (resolution pack), SM&CR (Senior Managers & Certification Regime), PS21/3 (operational resilience), and CBEST (cyber resilience testing). We also cover PRA requirements, Consumer Duty obligations, AML/CFT frameworks, and international equivalents across MiFID II, MAS, SEC, and other jurisdictions.
Reports can be used to support your firm's internal compliance processes, board reporting, and regulatory preparation. They are structured with FCA citations and formatted for executive and regulatory readability. However, Govisk reports are intelligence aids — not formal regulatory submissions. Any submission to the FCA should be prepared and reviewed by your qualified compliance function and legal advisors.
