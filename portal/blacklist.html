What is the FATF Blacklist? The Financial Action Task Force (FATF) identifies jurisdictions with strategic AML/CFT deficiencies. The Black List (High-Risk Jurisdictions subject to a Call for Action) requires enhanced due diligence and counter-measures. The Grey List (Jurisdictions under Increased Monitoring) signals active commitment to addressing identified deficiencies — enhanced due diligence is still required under the UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017 (Regulation 33). UK-regulated firms must apply Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for all transactions involving or connected to any listed jurisdiction.
