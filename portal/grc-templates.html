Full GRC software platforms provide automated workflows, pre-mapped frameworks (ISO, DORA, GDPR), continuous compliance monitoring, and audit-ready evidence logs — significantly reducing the compliance burden for FCA-regulated firms operating under SM&CR and CASS obligations.

Advisory — Selecting the Right GRC Approach for Your Firm
  • Early-stage / budget-limited: Start with free toolkits from Protecht or SureCloud to build your risk register and policy framework. Migrate to a platform when headcount and regulatory complexity justify it.
  • SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certification path: Vanta or Drata will significantly reduce audit preparation time and ongoing evidence burden — the platform cost is typically lower than the audit hours saved.
  • FCA-regulated firms (CASS, SM&CR, PS21/3): Riskonnect or Protecht enterprise platforms have pre-built modules aligned to operational resilience and board-level GRC reporting expectations.
  • Financial services with AML / KYC needs: Vobesoft offers a combined GRC + KYC solution, avoiding the cost of maintaining two separate platforms.
  • DORA compliance (effective Jan 2025): Vanta and SureCloud both offer DORA-specific readiness tools — essential for EU-regulated entities in scope.
  • Free Etsy templates: Suitable for initial documentation only. They do not fulfil FCA expectations for automated, evidenced, and auditable compliance programmes.
