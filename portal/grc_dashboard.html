Live Regulatory Updates
Key developments affecting GRC obligations — October 2026
SM&CR PHASE 1 REFORMS
Senior Managers & Certification Regime — Governance Reforms 2026
SM&CR Phase 1 governance reforms are live: April 2026 (policy statement), July 2026 (Recognised Investment Exchanges), September 2026 (FCA solo-regulated firms). Key change: COCON 1.1.7FR now explicitly covers non-financial misconduct as a conduct breach, significantly broadening individual accountability exposure for senior managers.
Effective: April – September 2026
PS26/2 — OPERATIONAL RESILIENCE
Operational Incident & Third-Party Reporting
PS26/2 establishes new mandatory reporting requirements for operational incidents and material third-party dependencies. Firms must notify the FCA of material operational incidents affecting important business services. Third-party providers (including cloud vendors) must be registered and monitored against new FCA expectations on concentration risk.
Effective: 18 March 2027
FCA GOVERNANCE EXPECTATIONS
FCA Portfolio Letter — Investment Management Governance 2026
FCA's Dear CEO letter (September 2026) identified inadequate board-level ownership of non-financial risk, poor escalation from second line, and overreliance on manual compliance controls as systemic weaknesses in the investment management sector. Firms are expected to evidence board-level GRC ownership and technology-enabled compliance monitoring by end of 2027.
Published: September 2026
