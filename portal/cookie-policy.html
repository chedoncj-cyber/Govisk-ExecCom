Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026  ·  Effective: 5 November 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device on subsequent visits and retain certain information across page loads. Cookies can be session-based (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent (retained for a set period).

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies we use on the Govisk ExecCom Intelligence platform, why we use them, and how you can control them. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

2. Cookies we use

We use a minimal number of cookies. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party profiling cookies.

Cookie Name Type Duration Purpose
cba Essential Persistent (localStorage) Records that you have acknowledged the cookie consent banner, so it is not shown again on return visits.
Session cookie Essential Session (browser close) Maintains your authenticated session while using the intelligence portal. Required for the platform to function correctly.

3. Essential cookies — no consent required

The cookies listed above are strictly necessary for the platform to function. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) and UK GDPR, strictly necessary cookies do not require your prior consent. Without these cookies, the portal cannot operate correctly.

We do not use any analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics), advertising cookies, or social media tracking pixels. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and you will be asked for explicit consent.

4. What we do NOT use cookies for

We confirm that we do not currently use cookies or similar tracking technologies for:

  • Behavioural or interest-based advertising
  • Cross-site tracking or profiling
  • Third-party analytics or audience measurement
  • Social media pixel tracking
  • A/B testing platforms

5. LocalStorage

We use your browser's localStorage (a web storage mechanism, not technically a cookie) to store your cookie consent acknowledgement (cba key). This is not transmitted to our servers and exists solely on your device. It can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

6. How to disable or delete cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may prevent certain features of the platform from working correctly. The following links provide guidance for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

To clear your cookie consent acknowledgement specifically, open your browser's developer tools, navigate to Application > Local Storage, and delete the cba entry for this site.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, particularly if we introduce new technologies or analytics. The "Last updated" date reflects the most recent revision. We will update the cookie consent banner if new non-essential cookies are introduced.

8. Contact

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at privacy@govisk.com.
