How It Works

From regulatory complexity to board-ready clarity

The WAT Framework — Workflows, Agents, Tools — separates regulatory knowledge from AI reasoning and deterministic execution, achieving high accuracy across complex multi-step intelligence generation.

01
Select your region and report type
Choose from 6 global regions and 13 intelligence report types — from CASS health checks and cyber threat assessments to country risk and executive protection briefings.
02
AI agents scan live regulatory data
Nine AI connectors simultaneously query authoritative regulatory repositories, enforcement databases, and geo-financial data sources — authenticated and cross-referenced in real time.
03
Intelligence is synthesised and structured
Findings are mapped to regulatory frameworks (FCA CASS, ESMA, MAS, SEC, SARB and others), RAG-rated for risk, and structured into board-ready narrative with full citation.
04
Report delivered — audit-ready in minutes
A structured .docx report with regulatory citations, risk ratings, and executive summary — ready for board submission, regulator response, or compliance filing.
